# Synthetic greenhouse notebook

This is invented sample material for trying Rust RAG. It contains no client records.

The Copper Finch greenhouse waters its tomato seedlings every two days. The volunteer checks soil moisture before watering and skips a watering when the soil is already damp.

The greenhouse uses a blue rain barrel beside the east door. The barrel holds 180 litres. A mesh cover keeps leaves out, and the volunteer rinses that cover each Saturday.

The Lantern team maintains the greenhouse. The Lantern team reports equipment problems to the Meadow workshop.
